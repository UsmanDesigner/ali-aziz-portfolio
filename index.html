About

Quality as a system, not a checkpoint

Six and a half years inside Pakistani pharmaceutical manufacturing — from in-process line clearance on the shop floor to owning quality management systems across contract manufacturing partners.

I started in 2019 doing in-process checks, line clearance and equipment qualification at Schazoo Zaka, and worked upward through Wilshire Laboratories and BF Biosciences into senior QMS ownership at CCL Pharmaceuticals. Today I manage Quality Assurance for Contract Manufacturing Organisations at Martin Dow Marker — auditing partner sites, reviewing quality agreements, and holding the QMS line on change control, CAPA, deviations and customer complaints.

The through-line in my work is evidence: a deviation that is properly root-caused, a CAPA that is actually effective, a batch record that tells the truth, and documentation that survives an inspector reading it cold. That mindset is what carried the CCL site through its PIC/S cGMP inspection readiness programme.

  • End-to-end QMS ownership — change control, deviation, CAPA, complaint and risk workflows
  • Audit-side experience on both benches: conducting CMO audits and preparing sites to be inspected
  • Comfortable in regulated data systems — SAP, Oracle ERP and digitised QMS platforms
  • Analytical toolkit for quality data: Minitab, SPSS, KPI and PQR trending
